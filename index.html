LESSON 1

Introduction to CAD

Understanding Computer-Aided Design and its role in engineering.

What is CAD?

Computer-Aided Design, commonly known as CAD, is the use of computer software to create, modify, analyse and document engineering designs.

CAD is widely used in mechanical engineering, manufacturing, civil engineering, architecture and industrial design.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the purpose of CAD.
  • Identify common engineering applications of CAD.
  • Understand the AutoCAD workspace.
  • Prepare for practical CAD drawing.

Practical Exercise

Open AutoCAD and identify the main areas of the interface: ribbon, command line, drawing area, navigation tools and status bar.
